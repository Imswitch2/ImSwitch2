Picasso — https://github.com/jungmannlab/picasso — Jungmann Lab, MPI of Biochemistry

Imswitch2 does NOT vendor or copy Picasso source code. Our SMLM detection and
fitting (imswitch/improcess/reconstructors/smlm/) are independent
implementations of the published Picasso approach (Schnitzbauer et al., Nat
Protoc 12, 1198-1228, 2017), written against numpy/scipy.

Picasso's licence is reproduced here anyway, because our implementation
follows their method closely enough that we would rather ship their terms
beside the acknowledgment than not. If anything here does turn out to be
derived from Picasso code rather than the published method, contact us and we
will attribute, relicense or remove it — see ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md.
